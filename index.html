Our Story

Founded by doctors.
Run like a clinic, styled like a lounge.

Zenith Nail Lounge was founded by Kelly Phan and Anthony Tram, doctors who graduated from the University of Medicine and Pharmacy in Ho Chi Minh City in 2004 and practised as ENT specialists before emigrating to the United States in 2010.

After more than 7 years in beauty care, they built the salon they could not find anywhere else: a place with a doctor's obsession with hygiene and a lounge's sense of occasion.
